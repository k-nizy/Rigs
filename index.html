Rotation Desk

Assign four operators and a task to each group of three rigs. The rotation is generated in the reference-sheet format, checked against the 6h / 1h / 1h budget, and handed to the rigs.

Rig Pedal Loop

One full-screen page, three foot pedals, no login and no task picker. It reads the pushed schedule and signs the right operator in when their turn starts.

apps/rig/

desk→ rigPayload()→ POST /api/schedules/push→ whoIsOn()→ the operator is signed in

The schedule lives in packages/engine/rotation-engine.js, which both apps load. It has no DOM and no globals, so it runs in the browser and under node alike — and the rig cannot drift from the desk that scheduled it. The desk is locked to the reference sheet: 15-minute blocks, 45-minute turns, each operator holding one rig until they step off. packages/engine/reference-sheet.test.js holds that sheet cell by cell and fails the build if the engine stops drawing it.

What the rigs send back goes to backend/ — an append-only ledger, from which every other table is derived. Episodes, faults, downtime and productivity, a floor board that notices the things nothing publishes, and a video path that lets a rig delete its own copy only once the bytes are verified.
