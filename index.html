Teleop Floor

Two screens and one schedule between them. The desk generates the rotation and pushes it out; each rig reads its own copy and already knows who is standing there. Neither can compute a different answer, because both run the same engine.

Rotation Desk

Assign four operators and a task to each group of three rigs. The rotation is generated, checked against the 6h / 1h / 1h budget, and handed to the rigs.

Rig Pedal Loop

One full-screen page, three foot pedals, no login and no task picker. It reads the pushed schedule and signs the right operator in when their turn starts.

rig/

desk-> rigPayload()-> rig/schedule.json-> whoIsOn()-> the operator is signed in

The schedule lives in shared/rotation-engine.js, which both apps load. It has no DOM and no globals, so it runs in the browser and under node alike — and the rig cannot drift from the desk that scheduled it. Defaults reproduce the reference sheet exactly: 15-minute blocks, 45-minute turns, each operator holding one rig until their break.
