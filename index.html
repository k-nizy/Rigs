How a shift runs

  1. The desk builds the rotation

    Four groups of three rigs, four operators each. One task per group for the whole shift, so nobody changes skill halfway through the day.

  2. One button puts it on the floor

    Every rig picks up its own copy. The desk checks first that each operator gets six hours of work, an hour of break and an hour of think time, and tells you if the shape does not come out even.

  3. The rig takes it from there

    It signs in whoever the schedule says is there, and hands over at the right minute — but never in the middle of a take. A recording always finishes first.
